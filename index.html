Biography

I began my research journey as a sophomore during my undergrad at DIU. While studying there, I participated in a semester exchange program at Dongseo University, South Korea. This experience strengthened my interest in deep learning and applied AI research. Throughout this journey, I have collaborated closely with research mentors and collaborators including Imrus Salehin (PhD Student, N.C.A&T, USA), Md. Baharul Islam (Associate Professor, FGCU, USA), and Dr. Fernaz Narin Nur (Professor, DIU). I have been recognized through key honors including the fully funded Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) in 2023 by the Government of the Republic of Korea and the Research Awards in 2024 and 2025 from the Division of Research, DIU. My research has been published in Elsevier, Wiley and Taylor & Francis. I was also recognized as a top performer in the Computer Vision and Deep Learning Bootcamp for Medical Data at DIU.
