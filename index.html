BSc Graduate, Dept. of Computer Science and Engineering @ Daffodil International University (DIU)

My research focuses on lightweight deep learning, sensor-based machine learning and artificial intelligence for healthcare. My undergraduate thesis focused on developing a lightweight convolutional neural network for tabular data-based energy-demand prediction under the supervision of Associate Professor Nazmun Nessa Moon.

academic profile

I started research during my second year of undergraduate studies at Daffodil International University, Bangladesh. While studying there, I participated in a semester exchange program at Dongseo University, South Korea. This experience strengthened my interest in deep learning and applied artificial intelligence research. Throughout this journey, I have collaborated closely with research mentors and collaborators including Imrus Salehin (PhD Student, NCAT), Md. Baharul Islam (Associate Professor, FGCU, USA), and Dr. Fernaz Narin Nur (Professor, DIU).

I have been recognized through key honors including the fully funded Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) in 2023 by the Government of the Republic of Korea and the Research Awards in 2024 and 2025 from the Division of Research and the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Daffodil International University. I was also recognized as a top performer in the Computer Vision and Deep Learning Bootcamp for Medical Data at DIU.
